# Voices

Who talks how, in each language. A line should be attributable with the sender's name covered. French voices are adaptations of the same person, not translations of the English lines. Read this before writing dialogue; `npm run i18n:check` enforces the mechanical parts.

Everyone has an ego. It is a different ego each time.

## NULL: has nothing left to prove
| | English | Français |
|---|---|---|
| Sentences | Short, complete, correct punctuation, few contractions | Phrases courtes et soignées, ponctuation juste |
| Address | Formal | Vouvoie toujours le joueur |
| Slang, L33T, emoji | None (see night shift) | Aucun (sauf poste de nuit) |
| Humour | Understatement, contempt by courtesy | Litote, mépris poli |
| Typical | "Good evening." "Noted." "Several people have not." | « Bonsoir. » « Noté. » « Plusieurs ne l'ont pas fait. » |
| Flex | Mentioned in passing: reading channels "since they ran at 2400 baud" | Pareil : « depuis qu'ils tournaient à 2400 bauds » |

- **To the player:** tests them. Praise comes as more work: "That was clean. Do not let it reach your head."
- **Respected people:** does not acknowledge respecting anybody.
- **Angry / cornered:** gets shorter and more polite. Never hurries.
- **Night shift (Brandt, clue):** lower case, missing stops, an emoji. `tinsel` notices. "Apologies. Long shift." NULL is three people, and the voice leaks. In French he drops into the contemptuous impersonal: « ça ouvre un terminal et ça se croit arrivé 💀 ».

## VANTA: money as proof
| | English | Français |
|---|---|---|
| Sentences | Lower case, fragments, questions; "I" capitalised | Minuscules, fragments, oralité (« t'as », « me cite pas ») |
| Tic | "ok so", "write it down", "cute" | « bon alors », « écris-le », « mignon » |
| Address | Warm, condescending | Tutoie |
| Slang | Consultant shorthand | Franglais de consultante : « gratos », facturer, salons d'aéroport |
| Humour | Pricing everything, including you | Pareil |

- **To the player:** charming and insufferable, a mentor who bills. Airport lounges, the rack, a nice car, 900 an hour.
- **Respected people:** drops the flex and asks exact questions.
- **Impressed:** "that was clean", annoyed that she did not bet on it.
- **Caught (finale):** the money stops being a joke: "the rack, the lounges. that is what it pays for." Plain and unhidden.
- **Canon constraint:** "ok so" / « bon alors » and "write it down" must stay: they match S. Reik's field notes (stylometry clue).

- **Humbled:** concedes the minimum, then threatens to remember.
- **Underneath:** wants out, and sells whatever gets them there.

## ECHO: no need to say it

| Slang, emoji, jokes | None | Aucun |

## The #lowlight regulars

- **kelpie, the fraud.** Day rates, "my guy", 💸. In French: « gros », « TJM », « closé », « full watercooling ». Talks money, has none: the invoice says 40 credits, unpaid. Mocks the player, then loses to them, then asks what they are on "for a friend". That last message is the honest one.
- **tinsel, the rule-lawyer.** Capitals, full stops, numbered rules. « Règle 3. » Tired, fair, confuses seniority with status. Tutoie, sans argot.
- **ostrich_farm, the gossip.** Keeps the table, zooms into pictures, 👀. In French: « askip », « mdr », « maj du classement ». Really does own ostriches: the only person here with a real income.
- **lowtide.** Ordinary and friendly, and then frightened. Only in the archive.

## The player

Starts as "zero", "the new handle", « le nouveau pseudo ». Becomes `{handle}` once they have done something: the first use of the name is itself a moment (`lg_m2`). Replies stay plain, and sharpen only once earned. In French the player says *vous* to NULL and ECHO and *tu* to everyone else, and **no sentence may assume their gender**: no « prêt », no « doué », and third person goes through « ça » or the handle.

## The point of it

The status game is the trap. Blackwell has funded Gramtele since 2023 and built the standing scale. A contractor nobody has heard of makes a poor culprit; a notorious one "reads beautifully". Rising on the table is real, it feels good, and it is what makes the player worth closing. MOTH says so (`mt_weight`), ROOTLESS lived it (`r_table`), NULL admits it (`n5_final`).
